House of Joshi: intentional meme artifacts

Character first. Expression and outfit establish the meme archetype. A chart or a short phrase adds the joke selectively; never both. Routine glow effects are removed; gold energy remains exclusive to the twelve Gold editions. Backgrounds support character identity rather than being chosen independently.

House mark: one small gold embroidered hallmark, never duplicate chest and headgear marks. Beanie, cap and cowboy hat are preferred; hoodie/puffer chest marks appear when headgear is not branded. The existing approved placement surfaces remain the only eligible ones. Headgear badges are 34–44 px; chest badge is 44–48 px on the full 1024px canvas.

Meme archetypes: Unbothered Holder, Reply Guy, Winter Holder, Chart Prophet, Terminal Degen, Lambo Dreamer, Moon Mission, Whale Council, Diamond Guard, plus Liquidity Pirate and the bespoke Genesis Prime. Archetypes are artistic categories, not promises of financial performance.

Approved Genesis/Vanguard/Main bracket quotas and twelve Gold editions are preserved. #1 remains the bespoke gold Genesis Prime with its approved gold body/headphones, determined expression, royal-starship background and blue puffer; its hallmark now follows the quieter scale. Selective clothing/headgear branding is preserved.

The mark is a visual hallmark, not a copy-prevention mechanism. Authenticity must be checked against the official Base contract address, token ID and provenance. This remains an unpublished local draft; existing production metadata and minted-token assignments are unchanged.
