Questions before liftoff.

Is this a collection or a drop?

The collection contains up to 1,200 Agent X-84 NFTs. The drop is the minting launch for that collection.

What is the mint price?

The initial price is 500,000 KABOOM. The owner can update it. Always use the live value shown before confirming.

Where does my payment go?

The full mint price goes to Development & Operations. The burn and reward are separately funded by the ecosystem budget.

Do I need to claim the reward?

No. The contract pays 50,000 KABOOM to the minter during a successful mint. There is no later reward claim or recurring holder payment.

Can I choose an NFT or rarity?

No. The contract assigns the next token ID. This is not a random selection mechanism, and the studio does not reserve artwork.

Where is my NFT?

Ownership is recorded on Base in the minting wallet. Wallets and marketplaces may need a metadata refresh or manual import.

Is minting ready?

The mint interface reads deployed contracts. Metadata hosting and marketplace display still need verification. A working contract button alone does not establish full launch readiness.

Are profits guaranteed?

No. Token burns and mint rewards do not guarantee price appreciation, resale demand or profit.

Are previews private?

Previews are removed from public navigation. Static website files and published metadata can still be accessed directly; this is not a concealed or random reveal.

Do I own the artwork copyright?

Current NFT holders receive personal, non-commercial display rights while they own their NFT. Copyright is not transferred, and merchandise, business branding and other commercial artwork uses require separate written permission. See NFT artwork rights for the full license.

Can support reverse a mint?

Support cannot reverse a confirmed blockchain transaction. Any applicable statutory rights remain unaffected; contact support with your public transaction hash.

How do I get help?

Email support@thehouseofjoshi.com. Do not send passwords, private keys or seed phrases.
