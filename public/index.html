A FEW THINGS TO KNOW
How do I get started?＋
Connect a browser wallet on Base, hold enough $KABOOM for the current mint price and ETH for gas, then review the mint. If needed, approve the exact token amount before confirming the NFT transaction. Minting requires a funded vault.
What does Connect wallet allow?＋
Connecting reads your account and network. Clicking mint and confirming the review can request token spending approval and an NFT mint transaction. Disconnect clears this page’s connection; revoke wallet permissions in your wallet.
Are rarity rankings and NFT rights published?＋
Artwork and local metadata have been prepared. Public metadata hosting and final licensing terms remain pending. Read the NFT rights notice.
Where can I verify the project?＋
The official Base token and NFT addresses are linked above. Community links, a trading pool and an independent audit have not been supplied.
Is this mint live?＋
This page connects to deployed Base contracts. The mint button is enabled only when contract data loads, supply remains and the NFT vault can fund the next mint. Check the live vault status above.
What happens to my mint payment?＋
The current mint price goes to the Development & Operations wallet. The 100,000-token split is funded separately from the ecosystem vault.
Does burning increase the floor price?＋
Burning reduces supply. Token prices and NFT floor prices depend on demand and market conditions; neither is guaranteed to rise.
